# 20. Hamiltonian mechanics: phase space and Hamilton's equations

- **Layer:** Classical mechanics
- **Depends on:** [19. Newtonian mechanics](19-newtonian-mechanics.md)
- **Used by:** [21. Properties of Hamiltonian flow](21-properties-of-hamiltonian-flow.md), [23. Canonical distribution and energy level sets](23-canonical-distribution.md)

## Role in HMC

Hamiltonian mechanics expresses the motion of a system as first-order equations in position and momentum, generated by one scalar function. The idealized proposal of HMC is the solution of these equations for a Hamiltonian built from the target density. All structural properties that make the proposal valid are properties of equations of this form.

## Required knowledge

- **Phase space.** The $2d$-dimensional space of pairs $z = (q,p)$ of position and momentum. A point of phase space determines the entire future and past motion.
- **Hamiltonian.** A function $H(q,p)$ that gives the total energy of the state. HMC uses the separable form $H(q,p) = U(q) + K(p)$ with $K(p) = \tfrac12 p^\top M^{-1}p$.
- **Hamilton's equations.** For $i = 1,\dots,d$,

  $$\frac{dq_i}{dt} = \frac{\partial H}{\partial p_i},\qquad \frac{dp_i}{dt} = -\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_i}.$$

- **Separable case.** For $H = U(q) + \tfrac12 p^\top M^{-1}p$, Hamilton's equations are $\dot q = M^{-1}p$ and $\dot p = -\nabla U(q)$. These equations equal the first-order form of Newton's law (block [19](19-newtonian-mechanics.md)).
- **Matrix form.** Let $J$ be the $2d\times 2d$ block matrix whose first block row is $(0,\ I)$ and whose second block row is $(-I,\ 0)$, where $I$ is the $d\times d$ identity matrix. Hamilton's equations are $\dot z = J\,\nabla H(z)$. The matrix $J$ is skew-symmetric, $J^\top = -J$.
- **Hamiltonian flow.** The flow $\varphi_t$ of the vector field $J\,\nabla H$ (block [03](03-ordinary-differential-equations-and-flows.md)).
- **Level sets.** The set $H^{-1}(E) = \lbrace z : H(z) = E\rbrace$ is the energy level set of energy $E$. A trajectory remains on the level set of its initial state (block [21](21-properties-of-hamiltonian-flow.md)).
- **Harmonic oscillator.** For $H = \tfrac12 p^2 + \tfrac12\omega^2q^2$ the level sets are ellipses in the $(q,p)$ plane, and each trajectory traverses one ellipse with period $2\pi/\omega$.
- **Status of the construction in HMC.** The potential energy $U$ is fixed by the target. The kinetic energy $K$ is chosen by the designer of the sampler. The time variable $t$ has no statistical meaning.

## Not required

- The derivation of Hamilton's equations from a Lagrangian by the Legendre transform.
- Canonical transformations, Hamilton–Jacobi theory and action–angle variables.
- Non-separable Hamiltonians, which arise only in Riemannian manifold HMC.
